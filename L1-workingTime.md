# 📝 Research: Labor Market and Digital Solutions

## 🏢 Selection of the 5 Companies
### **NotCo (Chile / Global)**

**Industry:** FoodTech / Artificial Intelligence.

**Why it stands out:** They use their own AI algorithm called “Giuseppe” to analyze molecular structures and recreate animal foods using plants.

**Expected stack:** Python (Data Science/AI), AWS.

### **Rappi (Colombia / Latin America)**

**Industry:** Delivery / SuperApp / Fintech.

**Why it stands out:** It is Colombia's first “unicorn.” They handle an impressive scale of real-time data and logistics, which requires a very robust backend.

**Expected stack:** Python (Data/Backend), Go, Node.js, Microservices.

### **Globant (Presence in Chile, Colombia, and Global)**

**Industry:** Software Development / Digital Consulting.

**Why it stands out:** They work with giant clients (Google, Disney, FIFA). They have specialized “Studios,” including one dedicated exclusively to AI & Data.

**Expected stack:** Python, Java, Cloud, Generative AI.

### **Mercado Libre (Leader in Latin America)**

**Industry:** E-commerce / Fintech.

**Why it stands out:** It is the most valuable technology company in the region. Its scalability challenges (millions of transactions per second) are the best place to learn high-level software architecture.

**Expected stack:** Go, Java, Python (Machine Learning), Big Data.

### **OpenAI (USA / Global)**

**Industry:** Generative Artificial Intelligence.

**Why it stands out:** Creators of ChatGPT. They are defining the future of programming and human-machine interaction.

**Expected stack:** Python (PyTorch, TensorFlow), Kubernetes, Azure.

## 🔍How did I find them?

I conducted research filtering by three key criteria: Technological innovation, Market presence (Chile/Colombia/USA), and Intensive use of data/AI.

For Chile and Colombia, I looked at rankings of “Unicorn” startups and “Best Place to Code” companies.

For the US/Global, I focused on companies that are leading the current technological frontier (GenAI).

## Why did I choose them?

I chose them because each one represents a different “level” or challenge for a career as a developer:

**NotCo** and **OpenAI** are purely applied scientific innovation (ideal for your interest in AI).

**Rappi** and **Mercado Libre** are schools of scalability and backend (ideal for learning how to manage massive systems).

**Globant** is the best option for diversity, allowing me to try different industries without changing jobs.

## Why would I like to work there?

Because in these companies, code isn't just maintenance; it's the core of the business. Working there would mean being exposed to complex problems (such as optimizing a delivery route in real time or training an AI model), which would dramatically accelerate my growth as an engineer. In addition, they often offer clear career paths and access to world-class mentors.

## What do they have that others don't?
**NotCo:** It has Giuseppe. It's not just software, it's science applied to biology.

**Rappi:** It has the speed of iteration. They implement changes very quickly; the pace is intense but very educational.

**Globant:** It has the “Studios” structure. It allows you to specialize deeply (e.g., become an expert in Python alone) instead of being a generalist.

**Mercado Libre:** It has the largest infrastructure in the region. Learning there is like playing in the “big leagues” of backend development.

**OpenAI:** It has exclusive knowledge. You work with technology that the rest of the world will only see in 6 months or a year.
